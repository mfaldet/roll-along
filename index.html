Roll Along: Marble Maze

A tilt-controlled marble game for iPhone.

Tilt your iPhone to roll a marble through hand-built mazes, dodge pits, collect coins and reach the goal. Play the Adventure, take on minigames like Roll Up and Snacky, collect cosmetics for your ball and board, and join a clan to compete in the monthly clan season.

Download on the App Store

Support

The quickest way to reach us is from inside the app:

  1. Open Settings from the Home screen.
  2. Tap Send Feedback.
  3. Pick Bug, Idea, Question or Compliment, write your message, and tap Send.

You can also email us at rollalongsupport@gmail.com.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. To restore purchases on a new phone, open Settings → Purchases → Restore Purchases. For refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Deleting your account

Signing in is optional. If you signed in with Apple, you can delete your account at any time:

  1. Open Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm, then confirm again when Apple asks.

Your account and its server data are removed right away, and Roll Along is signed out of Sign in with Apple. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Privacy

Roll Along does not track you and shows only non-personalized ads. Read the full Privacy Policy.
