# Every collection is a complete set

Every cosmetic collection now carries one of each slot: ball, goal, trail,
floor, pit, boundary and music. This file records what was missing and what
was added. `tools/cosmetic-calendar/bundle_rarity.py` prints the live table
(slots, rarity, full price) straight from `RollAlong/Cosmetics.swift`.

## 2026-11-20 — 18 complete, unique collections (Option 2)

Approved: keep about 18 collections on sale, each with exactly one item per offered slot (ball, goal, trail, floor, pit, boundary), and no cosmetic in more than one of them. Every other collection is **retired**: never listed or sold as a set, but its definition stays so owners keep what they have and its items keep their release days. Retired items stay buyable one by one (the Shop's rotating picks, and seasonal spotlights). Music is hidden in 1.0; each active collection keeps its old music entry for when music returns, and music is not part of the uniqueness rule.

Trails set the number: there are 17 coin-buyable trails plus the free default (then Graphite; now the Classic trail), so 18 was the most complete, unique collections the current art allows.

## What counts as a collection

- **Collections:** the 66 entries in `CosmeticBundle.catalogue`. That covers:
  - the permanent collections;
  - the 16 seasonal collections;
  - the launch gift collections;

  is complete once Aurora is.
- **Ball packs:** Planets, Sports Balls and Vintage Glass (`BallPack.catalogue`)

  they are unchanged.

## On-sale rule (November 20, 2026)

This replaces every earlier statement in this file that retired, seasonal or displaced pieces "stay buyable one at a time".

- A cosmetic can be seen and bought only if it belongs to a **full active collection**: all six slots filled (ball, goal, trail, floor, pit, boundary) and its release day reached. Pieces are still sold one at a time, but only pieces of such a collection.
- Every other piece is **held**: not shown, not sold, not announced. Held pieces keep their code and ids; an owner keeps them and can equip them from the Locker. The full list is in `docs/proposals/held-pieces.md`.
- Exempt (earned or purchase-only, never sold for coins): Diamond ball, Money Ball, Trophy ball, Money Roll trail, Money Full floor.
- Ball packs (Planets, Sports Balls, Vintage Glass Marbles) are held: each contains balls that are in no full collection.
- Every release is one or two full collections: Feb 11, 2027 Candyland and Nightclub; Apr 1 Golf and Bloom; Jun 3 Ocean. The loose release slots and the days that only brought pieces of retired sets are removed (database step 21 tidies the server table).
- A seasonal event sells no loose ball or boundary. While its window is open the Shop shows the one full collection it features.
- 22 collections are on sale, all full, plus the free Roll Along set.

## Audit against the quality standard (November 20, 2026)

After the quality round, every piece of the 22 collections on sale or scheduled was audited against `docs/cosmetics/quality-standard.md`, and every fail and borderline piece was fixed. The audit by collection, with verdicts and what changed, is in `docs/cosmetics/quality-audit-2026-11.md`.

- No collection price, tier or id changed.
- Eight pieces left their sets and are now held: Mosaic goal and Rainbow trail (Nightclub), Air trail (Golf), Parchment floor and pit (Bloom), Sky trail and Blueprint floor and pit (Ocean).
- Eight new pieces replaced them at the same tier: Vinyl goal, Dance Steps trail, Dew Line trail, Petal Path floor, Deep Rose pit, Wake trail, Open Sea floor, Reef pit.
- Held pieces now total 133; each has a verdict in `docs/proposals/held-pieces.md`.
