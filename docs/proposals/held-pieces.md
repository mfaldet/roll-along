# Held pieces: inventory for planning

Generated by `tools/cosmetic-calendar/held_inventory.py --write`. Do not edit by hand.

A cosmetic is on sale only as part of a **full active collection** (all six slots filled). Every other piece is **held**: not shown, not sold, not announced. Held pieces keep their code and ids, and a player who already owns one keeps it and can equip it from the Locker.

- Full active collections on sale: **22** (plus the free Roll Along set).
- Active collections that are NOT full (their pieces would be held): **none**.

## Exempt pieces (not held, never sold for coins)

| Ball | Money Ball | comes with the first 10,000-coin pack |
