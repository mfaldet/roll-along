# Collection overlaps: why "one collection per cosmetic" needs a decision

Status: **not built.** Requested 2026-11-19: no cosmetic may belong to more
than one collection, plus a few new collections from unassigned cosmetics.
With the cosmetics that exist today, that cannot be done while every
collection stays a complete set (the rule approved earlier: one ball, goal,
trail, floor, pit and boundary each). Nothing in the catalog was changed;
this note lays out the numbers and the choices.

## The numbers

66 collections (50 permanent, 16 seasonal). Each needs one item in each of
six offered slots. Buyable items per slot (free defaults and the
purchase-only Diamond, Money Ball, Money Roll and Money Floor left out):

| Slot | Buyable items | Collections needing one | Short by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trail | 18 | 66 | **48** |
| Floor | 28 | 66 | **38** |
| Pit | 28 | 66 | **38** |
| Goal | 32 | 66 | **34** |
| Boundary | 47 | 66 | **19** |
| Ball | 71 | 66 | 0 |

Even counting only the 50 permanent collections, trails are short by 32.

Today 86 items sit in two or more collections (music, which is hidden, not
counted). Examples: the Smoke trail is in 9 collections, the Gilded trail in
9, the Midnight and Velvet floors in 7 each, the Midnight pit in 7.

Unassigned items that could seed new collections: 9 Standard goals (Amethyst,
Bullion, Candy, Ember, Frost, Meadow, Slate, Vortex, Wormhole), the Galaxy
ball, the Ember pit, and the Ember and Obsidian boundaries. There are no
unassigned trails, floors or (beyond Ember) pits, so a new complete collection
can't be built without taking items from an existing one.

## Choices

1. **Unique pieces, sets no longer complete (recommended).** Each shared item
   stays only in the collection whose theme fits it best; the others lose that
   slot. Collections become 2–6 items, and each one's price drops by the items
   it gives up. Every item is then found in exactly one collection. The new
   collections you asked for would also be partial, built mostly from the
   unassigned goals plus pieces taken from overly shared sets. Side effects
   to approve: the seven-slot detail row shows only the slots a collection
   has; several collections may change rarity band, which changes the
   post-tutorial gift pool; collection release dates change for items that
   move.
2. **Complete sets, far fewer collections.** Keep about 18 complete,
   non-overlapping collections (limited by the 18 trails) and retire the rest
   from sale; their pieces stay buyable one by one. Owners keep everything.
   The calendar and seasonal windows would need reworking, including database
   rows.
3. **New art.** Keep every collection complete and unique by drawing about
   180 new items (48 trails, 38 floors, 38 pits, 34 goals, 19 boundaries),
   in code like today's. This is several rounds of work.
