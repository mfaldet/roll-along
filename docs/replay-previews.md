# Loadout and collection previews: recorded runs

The small "in action" previews (Profile → My Loadout, a collection's detail
card in the Catalog, and the Challenge Packs showcase on the game-mode screen)
play back a recording of a real Adventure run. No video and no physics: the
recording holds the ball's position every frame, the moments a coin, the goal
or a pit happened, and the level layout. Each preview draws it with its own
cosmetics: the viewer's equipped set on the Profile, the collection's set in
the Catalog and the showcase.

Every layer uses the game's own drawing: floor, pits, goal, coins, trail, ball
and boundary.

## The file

- Bundled recording: `RollAlong/PreviewReplay.json` (in the app target's
  resources).
- **The file in the repository today is a placeholder**, a hand-made path,
  not a real run. Replace it with a real recording.
- Size: about 9 KB for 6 seconds. A 20-second recording (the most the
  recorder keeps) is about 35 KB.
- Format (JSON): `version`, `fps`, `arenaWidth`/`arenaHeight` (play area in
  points), `ballRadius`, `coinRadius`, `level`, `holes` (pits, normalized
  rectangles), `goal`, `coins` (normalized points), `frames` (one
  `[seconds, x, y, roll]` per frame, x and y normalized 0 to 1), `events`
  (`{t, kind: coin|goal|pit, index}`).

## Making a recording

Only in a TestFlight or Xcode-installed build. An App Store build never shows
the recorder.

1. Open Profile. Press and hold the loadout preview.
2. Tap **Record next Adventure run**.
3. Go back and play an Adventure level (not level 1's first-time tutorial).
   Recording starts when the ball can move and stops at the goal, a fall, or
   after 20 seconds.
4. Back on Profile, press and hold the preview again. Tap **Use my recording
   on this phone** to see it in every preview. If you do not like it, tap
   **Record next Adventure run** again and replay.
5. When you like it, press and hold, tap **Share last recording**, and save
   it to Files or AirDrop it to your Mac. The file is named like
   `PreviewReplay-20261117T153000Z.json`; on the phone it is kept in the
   app's Documents folder.

## Swapping it into the app

1. On your Mac, rename the file to `PreviewReplay.json`.
2. Replace `RollAlong/PreviewReplay.json` in the repository with it (same
   name, same folder). Xcode already knows the file; nothing else to add.
3. Build. Every preview now plays your run for every player.

To go back to the bundled recording on your own phone, press and hold a
preview and tap **Use the built-in recording**.

## What to record

**Length:** play 8 to 12 seconds and end at the goal. The preview keeps the
last 6.5 seconds before the goal, plus a short hold for the goal burst, so
the loop is about 7 seconds.

**The stretch that looks best:** one smooth curve around a pit that passes
close to its edge, a coin picked up on the way, then a clean roll into the
goal. Why:

- A curve shows the trail best; straight lines hide most trails.
- A near-miss of a pit puts the pit style next to the ball.
- The coin shows a pickup, and the goal ends the loop with the goal burst.
- Keep it moving: a ball that stops reads as a still picture.
- Avoid wall bounces: the preview frames the stretch you played, so the
  screen edge you bounced off is usually outside the picture.

Pick an Adventure level with one pit between the start and the goal and a
coin beside it. Hold the phone so the ball rolls at a steady, medium speed.

## How the preview frames it

The camera is fixed for the whole loop. It fits the ball's path in the clip
(plus the goal) with room around it, at the preview's shape, and stays inside
the play area where it can. The ball, goal, coins and trail are drawn at the
game's own sizes and scaled with the camera, so they keep their in-game
proportions.

With Reduce Motion on, the preview shows a still frame just before the goal,
with the trail behind the ball.

The placeholder was generated by a small script, kept outside the repository:
it builds a smooth path around a pit, through a coin, into the goal, and
checks the path never touches a pit.
