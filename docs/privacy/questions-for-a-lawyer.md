# Questions for a lawyer before version 1.1 ships

Version 1.1 says the game is for players aged 9 and up, with one experience for everyone and no age question. That means the game knowingly has players under 13. The points below are the ones the code cannot settle. They are questions, not legal advice.

## 1. Does anything need a parent's consent first?

The game has no parental consent step. For a signed-in player it keeps an account identifier and a profile, and shows that profile to other players (friends, clans, leaderboards). For every player, signed in or not, it keeps gameplay records against a random number made on the phone, and Google's ad software receives technical identifiers.

- Do the account, friends, clans and leaderboards need verifiable parental consent for a child under 13? If so, what method?
- Do the anonymous gameplay records and the ads, as configured, fall within what is allowed without consent?
- Would it be simpler to keep under-13 players signed out? That would need an age question, which the owner has decided against.

## 2. Free-text feedback

Settings → Send Feedback lets anyone type up to 1,000 characters. A child could type a name, a school or contact details. It goes only to the developer and no other player sees it. Options are listed in the Stage 1 report. Is keeping free text acceptable, and with what limits?

## 3. Player search and friend requests

A signed-in player can search for other players by nickname and send a friend request to anyone. Nicknames are preset words, and there is no messaging. Is contact between strangers at this level acceptable for children?

## 4. Ads

Ads are rewarded ads from Google AdMob, requested with child treatment, a "General audiences" content cap, non-personalized, with location collection off and no advertising identifier. Is that enough, and must the policy name Google as a party that collects from children?

## 5. What the notice to parents must contain

The draft policy leaves these blank: the operator's legal name, mailing address and phone number; how many days a parent's request is answered in; how long feedback is kept.

## 6. App Store settings

- The age rating questionnaire in App Store Connect, to reach 9+.
- Whether to list the game in Apple's Kids Category. That category has its own rules on ads and analytics, so it is a separate decision from the age rating.

## 7. Outside the United States

If the game is sold in the EU, the UK or other places with their own rules for children's data, which of those apply?
