Privacy Policy — Roll Along: Marble Maze

Last updated: [date of publication]

Roll Along: Marble Maze (“Roll Along,” “the game,” “we,” “us”) is a marble game for iPhone for players aged 9 and up. Some of our players are children under 13. This policy is written for parents and guardians. It says what the game collects, why, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

The short version: the game works without an account and without telling us who you are. We do not ask for a name, an email address, a birthday, a photo or a location. We do not track players across other apps or websites, we show only ads that are not based on the player, and players cannot write messages to each other.

One experience for every player

We do not ask a player’s age. Every player is given the same protections we would give a child: the same ads settings, the same limits on what other players can see, and no way to type text that another player sees.

What we collect, why, and for how long

To count how the game is played, so we can fix problems and tune levels. It stays on the phone until the game is deleted. Records that carry it are deleted from our servers after 90 days.

To run the parts of the game that need an account: friends, clans and leaderboards. Until the account is deleted.
A game profile, only for signed-in players: a nickname built from two words chosen from our lists, the level reached, stars, scores, trophies, the chosen marble and color, friends, clan membership, and requests for lives sent to clanmates. Purchases. Apple handles every payment. We never receive a card number or any payment detail. Apple keeps the record that lets purchases be restored. Our gameplay records note that a purchase happened, with the product and the price shown. 90 days in our gameplay records. Apple’s own records follow Apple’s policy.

What we do not collect

Name, email address (any address Apple sends with a sign-in is removed within a day, as described above), phone number, home address, birthday or age, photos, videos, voice or other audio, contacts, precise or approximate location, health or fitness data, browsing or search history, or the device’s advertising identifier. The game never asks for permission to track.

Ads

The game shows ads only when a player chooses to watch one for an extra life. Ads come from Google AdMob. We set AdMob up for children for every player:

  • Every ad request is marked for child treatment, which tells Google to apply its protections for children and not to show ads based on the player’s interests or past activity.
  • Ad content is limited to Google’s “General audiences” rating, the most restrictive of its four ratings.
  • The ad software’s collection of approximate location is switched off.
  • The game never reads the advertising identifier.

To deliver an ad, limit how often it is shown and prevent fraud, Google receives technical information from the phone, such as an internet address, a device identifier that is not the advertising identifier, which ads were shown, and crash and performance data from its own software. What other players can see

Only signed-in players appear to others. Other players can see a signed-in player’s nickname, level, stars, scores, trophies, marble and clan. They cannot see anything a player has typed, because there is nothing to type: nicknames, clan names and clan descriptions are built from our lists, and clan chat uses fixed phrases. There are no private messages, no photos and no free-text profile fields.

Who we share information with

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and App Store purchases.
  • Google AdMob — the ads described above.
  • Supabase — the company that hosts our database. It stores the account, profile, gameplay records and feedback on our behalf.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own advertising.

For parents and guardians

You can at any time:

  • Delete your child’s account from inside the game: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes the profile, friends, clan membership, leaderboard standing, trophies and any feedback sent while signed in, right away, and signs the game out of Sign in with Apple.
  • Ask us to do it, or ask what we hold: email rollalongsupport@gmail.com. Tell us the nickname or friend code shown in the game so we can find the account. We will answer within [number] days and may ask a question to confirm you are the parent.
  • Stop further collection: signing out or deleting the account stops the account features. Deleting the game from the phone removes the random number and everything stored on the phone.

Gameplay records are tied only to the random number, not to a child, so we cannot look them up by name. They are deleted after 90 days in any case.
